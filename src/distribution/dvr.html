Stream, buffer and decode state, for working out why a
       picture is not what you expect.

    
The lit part of the timeline is held on this
       device: dragging there moves the real picture, frame for frame. Outside
       it you get a preview thumbnail while you drag, and the video arrives
       when you let go. The slider on the right sets how much of the window the
       bar covers — a narrower view is a finer drag.
